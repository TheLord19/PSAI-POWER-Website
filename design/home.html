What we design

Six practices that run from a feasibility study through to the moment a system is put into service.

Power system design & engineering

End-to-end design, from feasibility and concept through detailed drawings, equipment specification, and final implementation.

Electrical system protection

Protection schemes and relay coordination built so a fault clears where it should, and nowhere else.

Power system analysis & simulation

Load flow, short circuit, coordination, arc flash, and grounding studies modelled in ETAP and equivalent tools.

Renewable energy integration

Interconnection studies and design for solar, wind, hydro, and storage joining an existing grid — including DER connections to 150 MW.

Grid modernization & optimization

Upgrades to ageing infrastructure that raise capacity and reliability without rebuilding from zero.

Energy audits & efficiency studies

Measurement of where energy is actually being lost, and what it costs to recover it.
